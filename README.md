# Silicon Photonics Fundamentals 02 — Modulators and Detectors

Putting data on light and taking it off again: the plasma-dispersion effect, depletion, injection and MOS phase shifters, Mach–Zehnder and ring modulators, travelling-wave electrodes and drive energy, keeping rings on resonance, GeSi and lithium-niobate alternatives, germanium photodiodes and APDs, and the receiver that turns photocurrent into bits.

Topics: Plasma dispersion, MZM, Ring modulator, VπL, Ge photodiode, Sensitivity.

**Live site:** https://brendanjameslynskey.github.io/OptSiP_02_Modulators_and_Detectors/

Part of the [Silicon Photonics Fundamentals series](https://github.com/BrendanJamesLynskey/Optical_Hub_Silicon_Photonics). This is mostly a literature deck: every number is cited on the slide to a paper, standard or datasheet with a link, or computed live by the deck's interactive; vendor claims are labelled as the vendor's own and illustrative coefficients as illustrative.
